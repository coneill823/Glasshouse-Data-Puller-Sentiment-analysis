# Glasshouse privacy policy

> **Draft.** This describes what the website and the apps do, as built. Before
> publishing it, replace the two placeholders in *Who we are*, check the links,
> and put it at a public address: both app shops ask for one.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

## The short version

Glasshouse has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and we run no server
that receives anything about you. Your quiz answers never leave your device.

## Who we are

Glasshouse is published by **[your name or organisation]**. Questions about

Glasshouse is independent. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, any
parliament, government, political party or the organisations whose open data
it uses.

## What stays on your device

- **Your quiz answers**, which statements you marked as mattering a lot, and
  how far through the quiz you are.
- **The place you last looked up** — a postcode or a constituency — so that
  your representatives are there when you come back.

In the apps this is kept in the app's own private storage; on the website, in
your browser's local storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and it is
not backed up to us. Your answers say something about your political opinions,
which is why they are handled this way.

To remove it: choose **Clear my answers and start again**, or clear the app's
data, or uninstall the app. On the website, clear the site's data in your
browser.

## What leaves your device

- **A postcode, if you search by one.** It is sent over an encrypted
  connection to [postcodes.io](https://postcodes.io), an open postcode lookup
  service, which replies with the constituencies that cover it. We never
  receive it. Searching by the name of a constituency or a representative
  sends nothing. postcodes.io's own policy describes what it keeps.
- **Requests for newer voting data (apps only).** At most twice a day the app
  downloads the latest public data files from this project's repository on
  [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
  As with any download, GitHub sees the internet address the request came
  from. The request contains nothing about you or your answers.
- **Links you choose to open.** Links to the votes behind each figure open
  TheyWorkForYou or a parliament's own website in your browser. Those sites
  have their own policies.

If you use the website rather than an app, whoever hosts it will see the
ordinary record every web server keeps of the pages it sends.

## What we collect

Nothing. There is nothing to sign in to, and the apps contain no advertising
or tracking software.

## Children

Glasshouse is written for adults of voting age and is not directed at children.

## Changes

If what the apps do changes, this page will change with it, and the date at
the top will show when.
